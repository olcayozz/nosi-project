About

Nosi AI Lab is a research environment dedicated to exploring the potential of local large language models. We focus on making advanced AI technologies accessible and privacy-focused by enabling self-hosted solutions that put users in control of their data and AI experiences.

Our work centers around developing tools and methodologies that allow individuals and organizations to deploy, customize, and benefit from AI capabilities without relying on cloud-based services.
