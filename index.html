About Nosi AI Lab

Nosi AI Lab is a research environment dedicated to exploring the potential of locally-hosted Large Language Models (LLMs). Our mission is to democratize access to cutting-edge AI technologies by making them accessible through self-hosting solutions. We believe that with proper local infrastructure, powerful AI tools can be available to everyone, not just those with access to cloud computing resources.
