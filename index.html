About

Nosi AI Lab is a research environment dedicated to exploring the potential of local large language models. We focus on making advanced AI technology accessible and private by developing tools and platforms that run entirely on users' own hardware.

Our work includes optimizing model inference, creating autonomous agents, and building practical tools that empower individuals and organizations to leverage AI without compromising privacy or control.
