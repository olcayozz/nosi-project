About Nosi AI Lab

Nosi AI Lab is a research environment dedicated to advancing the field of local large language models. Our mission is to make AI accessible and empowering for everyone by developing self-hosted AI solutions that prioritize privacy, control, and accessibility.

Through our research and development efforts, we aim to create powerful AI capabilities that can be deployed locally, ensuring users maintain full ownership and control over their data and AI experiences.
