About

Nosi AI Lab is a research environment dedicated to exploring and developing local Large Language Models. We believe in democratizing AI by making powerful machine learning capabilities accessible through self-hosted solutions.

Our work focuses on creating efficient, privacy-preserving AI systems that can run on personal hardware, enabling users to harness the power of artificial intelligence without compromising their data.

By developing tools and frameworks for local AI inference, we're paving the way for a more decentralized and user-controlled artificial intelligence ecosystem.
