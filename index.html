About Nosi AI Lab

Nosi AI Lab is a research environment dedicated to developing and experimenting with local Large Language Models (LLMs). Our mission is to make advanced AI technologies accessible to everyone through self-hosted solutions that prioritize privacy and control.

We believe that the future of AI lies in democratizing access to powerful models while maintaining user privacy and data sovereignty. Our lab focuses on creating tools and frameworks that enable researchers and enthusiasts to explore AI capabilities locally.
