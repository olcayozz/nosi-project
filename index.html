About

Nosi AI Lab is a research environment dedicated to exploring the potential of local large language models. We focus on making advanced AI technologies accessible and privately hosted for individuals and organizations.

Our work centers around developing efficient inference methods, autonomous agents, and practical tools that enable powerful AI capabilities without compromising user privacy or data security.
