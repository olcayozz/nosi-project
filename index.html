About

Nosi AI Lab is a research environment dedicated to exploring the potential of local Large Language Models. We focus on making advanced AI accessible and privacy-focused through self-hosted solutions.

Our work enables individuals and organizations to run powerful AI models on their own hardware, maintaining control over their data and computations.

We believe that open, local AI technologies are crucial for the future of artificial intelligence as a public good.
